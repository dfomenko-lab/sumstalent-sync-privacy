Personal Google tools of Dmitry Fomenko

These are personal utilities used by a single person, Dmitry Fomenko, with his own Google account. They are not offered to other users.

  • Sumstalent Sync — copies candidate contacts from his recruiting database into his own Google Contacts and helps reconcile duplicates.
  • CSV to Google Contacts — imports contacts from CSV files on his computer into his own Google Contacts.
  • Hermes digest — a personal assistant on his private server that reads his Google Tasks, Calendar and Contacts to build a daily digest.

Privacy policy

Личные программы Дмитрия Фоменко для работы с его собственным аккаунтом Google: синхронизация контактов с рабочей базой, загрузка контактов из CSV и ежедневная сводка задач и календаря. Другим пользователям не предоставляются.
