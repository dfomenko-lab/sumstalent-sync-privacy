Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers the personal applications Sumstalent Sync, CSV to Google Contacts and Hermes digest. They are used only by their owner, Dmitry Fomenko (dfomenko@gmail.com), with his own Google accounts.

What Google data they use

  • Google Contacts (People API): reading contacts, creating contacts and adding phone numbers, e-mail addresses and links to existing contacts in the owner's account.
  • Google Tasks and Google Calendar (Hermes digest only): reading tasks and events, marking tasks done and creating events in the owner's account.

How data is handled

  • Data is processed only on the owner's own computer or on the owner's private server. It is not shared with or sold to third parties.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on those machines.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.
  • The applications' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
